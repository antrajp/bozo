SKU system — working principles
Keep it readable by category: leather / denim / jersey / outerwear as the primary tier, season and number as secondary. e.g. BZ-LJ-SS27-001.
Origin should be encoded or at least traceable — Japan vs. Morocco vs. France production matters for pricing, terms, and storytelling.
Consider a numbered edition flag for one-off leather pieces where the material won't repeat.
The system should sit comfortably on a line sheet from day one — categories clean, no ambiguity for buyers.
