Positioning summary
Heritage & legibility
A generational story that begins with Armand in Paris, rooted in Fez, and continues through Noam's relaunch. The work here is translation: making what already exists readable on first contact, so buyers can hold the story, not just touch the leather.
Production & proximity
Working in Fez is a return to origin, a personal connection to place and leather tradition, not a manufacturing convenience. That distinction matters. The closeness to the material is earned, which gives it a different weight than brands that simply choose an artisanal production region for positioning purposes.
The generational arc
Armand looked to the US the way his generation of makers did, as a source of a certain freedom in form. Noam looks to Japan with the same eyes: different era, different reference, same disposition. That parallel; father and son, each drawn to an outside influence - is the thread that connects the original Bozo to its relaunch. It's a brand built on admiration as method.
Craft & price
Material quality is confirmed at the point of touch. At designer price, that quality needs a frame — a reason the number feels inevitable rather than asserted. Visvim, Taiga Takahashi, Gajiroc: all occupy the same quadrant. All lead with story before garment. The quality is present. The narrative frame is the remaining work.
